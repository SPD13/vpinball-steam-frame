Create a backup

Download what you keep on the device to your computer, to bring it back after a new installation or onto another device.

    Restore a backup

    Choose a backup made from this page (.vpxbackup), then what to bring back. Files already on the device with the same size and date are skipped, so an interrupted restore can be started again.

      Restore this backup?

        Files of the device with the same name are replaced by the ones of the backup. Scores, players, favorites and table names are merged with the ones of the device. The settings of the device itself (displays, audio, controllers, folders) are kept.

        Keep the headset awake until the restore is over: it leaves the network when it sleeps.
